# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Funnel builders, designers, and GoHighLevel specialists use Website Storage to study landing pages by industry and open working previews.

## Product Purpose

The library holds standalone fictional service-business demos. The salon, residential roofing, and dog daycare pages show three distinct paths from researching a service to trying its first conversion step.

## Brand Personality

The gallery is a practical reference tool. Each demo has its own voice, visual identity, and way of organizing the decision.

## Anti-references

Avoid repeating a common landing-page layout, copying reference sites, or inventing customer proof.

## Design Principles

- Give each industry a distinct first screen and interaction.
- Clearly identify simulated requests and fictional businesses.
- Make demos easy to find through their niche filters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support narrow viewports, keyboard operation, visible focus, clear labels, and reduced motion.
